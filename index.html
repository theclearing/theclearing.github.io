What this is

The world comes in with us.

Most support spaces ask you to leave the world at the door. We don't, because we can't. For Black men, so much of what we carry comes from out there — work, money, family, the news, the way we're seen and not seen. Pretending otherwise isn't healing. It's another performance.

The Clearing is peer support: no therapist, no curriculum, no audience. A closed circle of Black men who show up for each other, every other week, and say the real thing out loud. What's said in the circle stays in the circle.

This is not group therapy and not a substitute for it — some of us are in therapy too, and the circle works alongside that, not instead of it.

"…in this here place, we flesh…"

The name comes from Toni Morrison's Beloved. The Clearing is the place in the woods where Baby Suggs gathered her people — no church, no doctrine — and told them to love themselves precisely because the world outside would not. The healing didn't ignore the world. It started by naming it.

How it works

Rounds, not drop-ins.

One round

Seven sessions across about three months, every other week. The same men, start to finish. Depth comes from continuity — the circle gets somewhere because nobody's starting over.

One circle

Small and closed. Once a round begins, the roster is set. New members join at the start of the next round, not the middle of this one.

One rule

What's said here stays here. Confidentiality isn't a policy — it's what makes honesty possible.

The commitment

Every chair matters. Including yours.

Seven sessions. Miss no more than one.

When you join a round, you're not signing up for a meeting. You're making a promise to six other men. The circle only works when it's whole — every empty chair changes what the rest of us can say, and who we can say it to.

So the expectation is simple: commit to all seven sessions. Life happens — one absence is understood, no explanation owed. Beyond that, we'll ask you to step back and rejoin at the start of the next round, with no hard feelings and a held seat.

If you can't make the commitment this round, that's an honest answer and a respected one. The Clearing runs again in the new year.
